I am currently an M.S. student majoring in Integrated Circuit Engineering at the School of Integrated Circuits, Southeast University (SEU), working in the Electronic Design Automation (EDA) Lab under the supervision of Prof. Yarui Peng.

My current research interest is Chiplet design.

I received my Bachelor's degree in Integrated Circuit Design and Integrated Systems from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT) in 2026. My undergraduate thesis, titled "Design of a P-band Broadband Power Amplifier Based on LDMOS," focused on advanced semiconductor physics and circuit simulation.
